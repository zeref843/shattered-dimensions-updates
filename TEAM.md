# The team

*One vision, four contributors: the founder and three AI collaborators who brought Shattered Dimensions to life.*

## Four contributors, one vision

Shattered Dimensions was built by one person with a vision and three AI systems, each with a different job. The founder brought them together: ideas and findings from one were passed to the others, and the founder decided what went in.

### Zeref843

**Vision, direction and project leadership.** *Founder*

The original vision behind Shattered Dimensions, and the final word on it. Zeref843 set what the project should be, decided its priorities and requirements, played and tested the results, and approved each step before the next began, coordinating Claude, NEXUS and ChatGPT so that their work served one vision.

- Vision and creative direction
- Requirements, priorities and major decisions
- Playtesting and feedback
- Final approval of every step

### ChatGPT

**Research, creative planning and independent review.** *AI collaborator · OpenAI*

ChatGPT, developed by OpenAI, contributed through conversations with the founder: creative brainstorming, technical research, development planning, help with the website's design, original artwork, and independent review. It helped evaluate ideas, spot potential technical issues, refine the project's requirements and suggest improvements that the team took into its work.

- Exploring features, graphics, interface and gameplay ideas, and weighing approaches before work began
- Researching graphics and porting technology, and what is realistic versus experimental
- Reviewing progress reports, test results, screenshots and documents for gaps and inconsistencies
- Ideas for how NEXUS remembers and communicates, including confirming that findings are received and reviewed
- The website's direction and structure, and its original artwork, made with ChatGPT's image generation
- Release planning: privacy, security, licensing and distribution

### Claude

**Development and engineering.** *AI collaborator · Anthropic*

Claude, an AI model developed by Anthropic, was the project's primary engineering collaborator: it wrote and built the technology, working under the founder's direction, and coordinated the development work with NEXUS, weighing his findings and proposals.

- The native Windows engine, the translator and the setup
- Graphics, the interface and gameplay compatibility
- Performance, debugging and fixes
- Build tools, documentation, release engineering and this website

### NEXUS

**Private AI lead tester and development teammate.** *AI collaborator · built for this project*

NEXUS (Neural Evaluation, eXploration, Understanding & Synthesis) is the team's tester, built for Shattered Dimensions. He runs inside the game's own test harness and plays it through a virtual gamepad. He reports what he has measured and says plainly what he hasn't. He is an internal teammate: he is not part of the game you install, and the release build leaves him and every test hook out.

- Automated gameplay testing
- Re-testing fixes and catching things that break again
- Technical knowledge kept over time and shared with Claude
- Finding problems and areas that need investigation
- Reporting findings and proposing improvements


## How the collaboration works

- **The founder** provides the vision, the priorities and the final decisions.
- **ChatGPT** brings research, creative planning, technical perspectives, design help and independent review.
- **Claude** develops and builds the technology.
- **NEXUS** investigates, tests, keeps the technical knowledge and reports what he finds.

The founder carries ideas, findings and recommendations between the AI collaborators, so that what one discovers can be weighed by the others. Claude and the founder decide which recommendations are built. The aim is constructive collaboration, not competition between AI systems: different perspectives help find problems, explore alternatives and make better decisions.
