# Coming soon

*Ideas we are working toward. None of these exist yet.*

## Planned for the community

These are plans, not features. Nothing on this page is available today, and none of it collects or uploads anything. When one arrives, it will be announced on [Updates](UPDATES.md).

### Mod browser

*Planned.* Find texture packs and mods made by the community, in one place, with what each one changes.

### Sharing your mods

*Planned.* A safe way for creators to publish their packs, with checks that no game files are included.

### Mod management

*Planned.* Install, update and switch mods from inside Shattered Dimensions.

### Creator tools and SDK

*Planned.* More ways to build for Shattered Dimensions, with documentation for creators.

### Texture tools

*Planned.* Better tools for finding, painting and testing textures for your packs.


## Making packs today

You can already make and share texture packs, including ones that restyle the new interface: see the [texture pack guide](docs/handbook/texture-packs.md).
