# Support

*Fix a problem step by step, or report a bug.*

## What is the problem?

Most problems have a known fix: see [Troubleshooting and FAQ](docs/handbook/troubleshooting.md) first.

On [the website](https://shattereddimensions.com/support.html), a guided helper shows the fixes for each kind of problem and fills in a report for you. Here on GitHub you can [open a report directly](https://github.com/zeref843/shattered-dimensions-updates/issues/new/choose): choose **Bug report** and fill in the form.


## Before you report

- **Reports are public.** They are posted as GitHub issues, which anyone can read. Do not include your name, email address, files from your PC, or anything personal.
- **Never attach game files**, dumps or keys.
- A GitHub account is needed to post. Nothing is sent until you press GitHub's own button.
