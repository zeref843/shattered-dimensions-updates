# Interface and controls

## The one-screen layout

On the 3DS the game used two screens. Shattered Dimensions shows everything on one:

- **The game** fills the window, in widescreen 16:9 or the original 5:3.
- **The hearts** sit at the top left and **the rupee counter** at the top right. The **energy gauge** sits under the hearts as a horizontal bar, and grows longer with the Stamina Scroll.
- **The minimap** (the game's own live map) sits in a corner, bottom left by default. Next to it are the **Gear** and **Items** buttons, and the items you have on **Y** and **X**.
- During cutscenes and dialogue the whole HUD fades out, exactly when the game hides its own hearts.

**F9** switches to the classic two-screen layout and back.

In the settings window (**F10**, **Minimap and HUD**) you can change the minimap's size (small, medium, large), its opacity (30 to 100%), its corner, and the HUD's overall scale (70 to 150%).

## The map

Open the map with **M**, by clicking the minimap, or with **R3** on a controller. It opens as a panel; press **Y** for full screen (and again for the panel). Everything the original map does works here, through the game's own map:

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Open or close the map | M, click the minimap, Esc to close | R3, B to close |
| Move the cursor | the mouse | either stick |
| Touch (the zoom button, pins, floors) | click | A or R3 |
| Drag a pin or the zoomed map | hold the button and move | hold A or R3 and move |
| Zoom in or out | mouse wheel, or C and Z | right and left trigger |
| Panel or full screen | Y key (U by default) | Y |

Beside the map panel, **Map Information** names the place you are in, in the game's own words.

## Items and Gear

- **Items:** press **T**, click **Items**, or push the controller's right stick to the right.
- **Gear:** press **G**, click **Gear**, or push the right stick to the left.
- **Close:** **B**, **Esc**, or click outside the panel.

Both open as panels with an information box beside them: the item or piece of gear under the cursor, with its picture, name and description, and the items on Y and X.

## Choosing items

There are three ways to put items on **Y** and **X**. Choose yours in the setup or in the settings window (**F10**, **Equipment selection mode**); you can switch at any time.

| Mode | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Hold** | Hold the left trigger (or **Z**) to choose for Y, or the right trigger (or **C**) to choose for X. A strip of your items appears; move along it and release to equip. |
| **Wheel** | The same holds open a radial wheel of eight items per page; point with the stick or mouse and release. **L** and **R** (or **Q** and **E**) change page. |
| **Classic** | A press opens a window of all your items; press **Y** or **X** (or click with the left or right mouse button) on an item to equip it there. |

In every mode, **B** cancels. In Hold and Wheel, releasing on the item already equipped changes nothing.

## Default controls

| The original's button | Controller | Keyboard and mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Circle pad (move) | Left stick | W A S D |
| A / B / X / Y | the buttons in the same positions as on the 3DS | K / J / I / U |
| L / R | LB / RB | Q / E |
| Start / Select | Start / Back (View) | Enter / Backspace |
| D-pad | D-pad | Arrow keys |
| Choose an item for Y / X | Left / right trigger | Z / C |
| Touch screen | Right stick moves a cursor, R3 taps | the mouse, inside a panel |

Always available: **F10** (settings), **F9** (one or two screens), **Alt+Enter** (window or full screen).

The buttons the game shows on screen ("Press A" and the like) follow the device you used last: Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo symbols, or your keyboard keys.

## Remapping and controllers

In the settings window (**F10**), the controls section lets you:

- see your connected devices;
- remap any action by pressing the new key or button (two bindings each), with a warning if two actions clash;
- keep separate layouts for the keyboard, for all controllers, or for one specific controller;
- set the left stick's inner and outer dead zones, response curve and sensitivity, with a live view;
- set the rumble strength (the controller rumbles when Link is hurt) and test it.

Any number of controllers can be connected; the one you used last is the active one, and you can plug and unplug them while playing. Supported controllers include Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock 4 and DualSense), Nintendo Switch Pro and Joy-Con, 8BitDo, PowerA and the many others Windows and SDL recognise as gamepads. Steam Input works if you turn it on but is never needed.

> So far the controls have been tested with virtual controllers and the keyboard. If your controller does not work, or is not recognised, please tell us through [Support](../../SUPPORT.md): an unknown controller with no built-in mapping is not used yet.

## Saving

The game saves exactly as the original does: at weather vanes and when you choose to save. Shattered Dimensions adds safety around that:

- each save is written completely or not at all, so a crash cannot corrupt it;
- the files live in `%APPDATA%\Shattered Dimensions\saves`, apart from the game;
- updates back them up first and keep them.

Our start menu shows your three files with what each one carries, and lets you **play, copy or delete** a file. Copying asks where to, and confirms before replacing a file; deleting asks first, with Cancel as the default.
